### 🔬 Freelance & Consulting Services

* **16S rRNA & Shotgun Microbiome Analysis:** End-to-end processing from raw FASTQ quality control and ASV denoising (QIIME 2 / DADA2) to alpha/beta diversity, differential abundance (MaAsLin2, LEfSe), and functional pathway prediction (PICRUSt2).
* **Single-Cell RNA-seq (scRNA-seq):** Preprocessing, batch correction, dimensionality reduction, clustering, cell-type annotation, and differential expression analysis (`Scanpy`, `scVI-tools`, `Seurat`).
* **Biostatistics & Machine Learning:** Feature selection (`Boruta`), multivariate modeling (`db-RDA`), and predictive modeling for clinical and epidemiological datasets.
* **Reproducible Pipelines & Reporting:** Automated Bash/R/Python workflows with clean HTML/Markdown reports and high-resolution, journal-ready figures.
